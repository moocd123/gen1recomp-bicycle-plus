# Contributing

Bug reports and compatibility reports are welcome.

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Gen1ReComp++ version.
- Pokémon edition (Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver or Crystal).
- Device / operating system.
- Bicycle Plus version.
- Other enabled mods and their versions.
- Colour mode if the problem is visual.
- Clear reproduction steps.
- A screenshot or short video where useful.

Please do not upload ROMs, save files containing personal information, or copyrighted game data extracted from a ROM.
